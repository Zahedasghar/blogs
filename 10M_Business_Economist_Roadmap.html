Strategic Business Plan 2025-2029

Maximizing Impact in Pre-Retirement Years & Beyond

Author

Prof Dr. Zahid Asghar

Published

October 19, 2025

1 Executive Summary

This strategic plan outlines a roadmap for Prof Dr. Zahid Asghar to maximize professional impact and income over the next 4 years (2025-2029) leading to retirement at age 60, and establish sustainable post-retirement revenue streams.

Core Objective: Generate PKR 800,000-1,500,000 monthly income through diversified consulting, training, and digital products while building a flexible post-retirement business model.

Timeline: 30-day launch → 60-day scaling → 6-month sustainable model → Post-retirement transition

2 Your Unique Market Position

2.1 Core Credentials

  • Professor of Economics, School of Economics, Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad
  • Former Leadership: Director, Registrar (administrative excellence)
  • Specialized Expertise: Development Economics, Pakistan Economy, Applied Econometrics, Economic Statistics
  • Technical Skills: R Programming, Data Modeling, AI Applications, Impact Evaluation
  • Training Track Record: 2+ years conducting AI and data analysis workshops
  • Academic Impact: Supervised 7 PhD candidates, published in national/international journals
  • Thought Leadership: Evidence-based policy making, numerical/visual literacy advocate

2.2 Competitive Advantages

Why You Stand Out
  1. Rare Combination: Academic rigor + practical R/AI skills + Pakistan economy expertise
  2. Credibility: QAU professorship provides institutional legitimacy
  3. Policy Connection: Understanding of government, NGO, and development sector needs
  4. Bilingual Capability: Can deliver in Urdu and English
  5. Proven Training Model: 2 years of workshop experience to refine
  6. Network Access: Academic, government, and international development connections

2.3 Market Gaps You Can Fill

  • Evidence-based policy analysis training (severely underserved in Pakistan)
  • R and data modeling for non-technical decision makers
  • AI literacy for government and policy organizations
  • Pakistan economy analysis with rigorous statistical methods
  • Impact evaluation methodology for development programs

12 Frequently Asked Questions

12.1 About Time Commitment

Q: Can I do this while still working full-time at QAU?

A: Yes. The model is designed for 10-15 hours/week initially for business development, with most consulting/training on weekends or evenings. As it scales, you hire help and systematize.

Q: What happens to university work?

A: Continue as normal. Many professors do consulting. Key is to maintain ethics: no conflicts of interest, fulfill QAU commitments first, no use of university resources for private work without permission.

12.2 About Clients

Q: Where will clients come from?

A: Multiple sources: - Your existing professional network (colleagues, former students, conference contacts) - LinkedIn outreach and content marketing - Direct proposals to organizations - Partnerships with training companies - International organization RFPs - Referrals from satisfied clients

Q: What if clients dry up?

A: Diversification protects you. Multiple streams, multiple client types, multiple geographies. If corporate slows, NGOs may be busy. If Pakistan market is slow, international projects. Plus digital products provide baseline income.

12.3 About Competition

Q: Aren’t there many data science consultants already?

A: Your competitive advantage is unique: - Academic credibility (QAU professor) - Pakistan economy specialization - Development sector understanding - Evidence-based policy expertise - Bilingual capability - Proven teaching track record

You’re not competing with general data scientists. You’re the “Pakistan economic policy + data” expert.

Q: What about younger, cheaper consultants?

A: You compete on value, not price. Senior clients (government, NGOs, think tanks) value experience, academic rigor, and reputation. They want someone with PhD-level expertise. Your pricing reflects this.

12.4 About Scaling

Q: How do I scale beyond my personal time?

A: Multiple approaches: 1. Hire teaching assistants to deliver programs 2. Create digital products (sell while you sleep) 3. Partner with institutions (they deliver your curriculum) 4. Build retainer model (efficient recurring revenue) 5. Eventually license your methodology

Q: Can this become a real firm?

A: Yes. Over 5-10 years, you could build “Evidence-Based Policy Consultants” into a recognized firm with multiple consultants, standardized offerings, and significant revenue. But that requires different decisions (management vs. consulting). For now, focus on profitable solo practice.

12.5 About Post-Retirement

Q: Will there be demand for a 60+ consultant?

A: Absolutely. Your value increases with experience in consulting/advisory work. Organizations value wisdom, judgment, and depth. Plus, retainer clients who’ve worked with you for years won’t drop you at 60. Digital products have no age limit.

Q: What if I want to retire completely at some point?

A: The model is designed for eventual complete passivity: - Digital products run themselves - Licensed curriculum generates royalties - Could sell the business/brand - Or simply wind down client work gradually

Q: Can I make this family income, not just supplemental?

A: Yes. By month 6, PKR 800,000-1,500,000/month exceeds most Pakistani household needs. It’s designed as primary income post-retirement. The pension from QAU becomes bonus.
