₨10 Million Roadmap for a Data Economist

8–12 month execution plan for monetizing econometrics, data analytics, and impact evaluation

Author

Zahid Asghar

Published

October 18, 2025

Note

Goal: Earn ₨10 million within 8–12 months by productizing expertise in econometrics, data science, and impact evaluation for clients in Pakistan and the Global South.

1 Executive Summary

Strategy: Build a data-first business ecosystem around your authority as an econometrician & evaluation expert. Combine high-ticket consulting with scalable training and digital assets.

Target Mix (12 months)

Stream Description Revenue Target
Consulting & Impact Evaluations Policy research, RCTs, survey analytics, dashboards ₨5.0M
Training & Courses R/Stats/ML workshops, institutional training, cohorts ₨2.0M
Analytics Products Streamlit/Shiny dashboards, forecasting tools, indices ₨1.5M
Sponsored Briefs & Publications Paid policy notes, commissioned Quarto reports ₨1.0M
Speaking & Mentorship Talks, advisory retainers ₨0.5M
Total ₨10.0M

North Star: Monetize credibility and reusable assets, not just hours.

2 30-Day Plan — Foundation & Visibility

2.1 Positioning

  • Revamp LinkedIn/X bios: Econometrician · Data Scientist · Impact Evaluation · AI for Policy.
  • Launch/refresh personal Quarto site with: About · Portfolio · Datasets · Dashboards · Contact.
  • Publish 1 signature article: “From Data to Policy: A Practical Playbook for Pakistan.”

2.2 Offers

Create three clear offer pages with outcome-driven language and price anchors: - Impact Evaluation & Policy Analytics — RCT/DiD/IV, survey & M&E frameworks (₨8–15 lakh / project). - Capacity Building — 2–3 day bootcamps in R/Stats/AI for policy teams (₨3–6 lakh / cohort). - Analytics Products — Dashboards, forecasting models, indices (₨2–5 lakh / build).

2.3 Trust Assets

  • 3–4 data visuals: exports map, remittances trends, trade-gap explainer, poverty heatmap.
  • 2 case-style writeups (problem → data → method → insight → impact).

2.4 Outreach

  • Build a list of 50 warm contacts (donors, ministries, think tanks, telecoms, NGOs).
  • Send 15 personalized notes/week + 1-page capability deck.
  • Offer 1 free mini-brief or 45-min scoping call per org.

3 60-Day Plan — First Wins & Proof

3.1 Revenue Targets (by Day 60–75)

  • ₨300–400k / month via 1–2 paid engagements (analysis or evaluation design).
  • Launch 1 paid workshop (R for Policy Analytics or Impact Eval with Stata/Python).

3.2 Deliverables

  • Reusable Do-File/Notebook library (DiD, IV, RDD, PSM, FE/RE, panel TS).
  • Draft Monitoring & Evaluation Toolkit (templates: ToC, logframe, sampling, survey spec).

3.3 Visibility

  • Publish 2 data briefs (Quarto): Trade Gap, Education Outcomes Tracker.
  • Start a monthly newsletter: AI & Economics — Pakistan Edition.

4 90-Day Plan — Scale & Systemize

4.1 Productize

  • Ship one mini-product:
    • Pakistan Trade Pulse (Streamlit) or AI Policy Readiness Index (Shiny).
    • License access: ₨1–2 lakh / client (think tanks, journalists, investors).

4.2 Scale Consulting

  • Create sector modules (Education, Health, Energy, Digital Economy) with methods, datasets, price cards.
  • Hire 1 RA (data cleaning + viz + QA) to increase throughput.

4.3 Passive Streams

  • Record 6–8 lectures → self-paced course (Udemy/Thinkific/atomcamp).
  • Bundle datasets + reproducible code.

5 Months 4–12 — Multiplying Income

5.1 High-End Projects

  • Bid national studies (AI readiness, digital economy, welfare targeting).
  • Act as local partner for international consultants (WB/ADB/UN). Ticket size: ₨1.5–3M each.

5.2 Sponsored Publishing

  • 1 policy brief/month (sponsored at ₨100k–150k). Distribution: Quarto → LinkedIn → X → SSRN.

5.3 Training Brand

  • “Econometrics for Evidence-Based Policy” — quarterly cohorts + institutional bootcamps.
  • Aim for ₨1M/month run-rate by Month 10–12.

6 Pipeline, Pricing & Packages

6.1 Flagship Packages

  • Evaluation Accelerator (8–12 weeks) — design → sample → instruments → analysis → policy brief.
    Price: ₨1.2–1.8M
  • Data Modernization Sprint (6 weeks) — data audit → cleaning → dashboards → training.
    Price: ₨600k–900k
  • Policy Brief Series (per brief) — 12–18 pages with viz & code repo.
    Price: ₨100k–150k

6.2 Rate Anchors

  • Daily: ₨80k–120k · Weekly: ₨350k–500k · Monthly Retainer: ₨900k–1.3M

7 Outreach Operating System

Weekly Cadence - 10 warm intros, 5 follow-ups, 2 discovery calls, 1 proposal, 1 public artifact.

Email Pitch (template) > Subject: Rapid evaluation support for {{org}}’s {{program}}\n\nDear {{Name}},\n\nI specialize in econometric evaluation and policy analytics (DiD/IV/RDD, R/Stata/Python).\nI reviewed {{program}} and see opportunities to improve evidence and decision speed.\n\nCould we explore a 45-minute scoping call? I can share a 1-page approach and sample deliverables.\n\nBest,\nZahid

8 Content Engine

Monthly Content Plan - 1 flagship brief, 2 data visuals, 1 tutorial thread, 1 founder-style note. - Topics: trade, inflation, remittances, education outcomes, AI for governance.

9 KPIs to Track

Area KPI Target
Pipeline Discovery calls / week 2–4
Conversion Proposal → Win rate 25–40%
Visibility New followers / month 400–600
Product Licenses sold / quarter 3–6
Finance Gross monthly revenue ₨0.8–1.2M by Month 10

10 Risks & Mitigations

  • Delivery Risk: Overcommitment → Use RA + standardized templates + weekly reviews.
  • Client Dependence: Diversify across sectors & mix training + products.
  • Pricing Pressure: Offer tiered options; publish case studies to defend value.
  • Regulatory/Payments: Use Wise/Payoneer for foreign clients; keep invoices compliant.

11 Tooling Stack

  • Authoring: Quarto, LaTeX, Canva · Data: FAOSTAT, PBS, SBP, WAPDA, PSX
  • Ops: Notion, Google Drive, GitHub, Zapier · Comms: LinkedIn, X, Zoom
