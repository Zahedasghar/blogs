AI Literacy for Public Policy Officials

Workshop for Punjab Government

Zahid Asghar | Quaid-i-Azam University

🎯 Workshop Goals

  • Build AI literacy among Punjab Government officers
  • Demonstrate 15 practical GenAI prompts for public policy, health, education & operations
  • Emphasize safe, ethical, and efficient AI use in government
  • Practice hands-on with real department scenarios

🏛 Section 1: Public Policy & Governance

1. Policy Problem Framer

You are a senior public-policy advisor in Government of Punjab.
Define the core policy problem I describe. List 3 root causes, 3 departments that must coordinate, and 2 risks if no action in 12 months.

Problem: [type issue – e.g., rising smog episodes in Lahore].

Try it: Use your own current policy challenge

2. Three-Option Policy Brief

Prepare a short note for Secretary P&D giving 3 options – minimal change, moderate reform, aggressive reform – for [topic].
For each option include estimated cost (low/med/high), timeline (months), and political risk.

Tip: Add “consider Punjab’s fiscal constraints” for realistic options

3. Summarize for Secretary

Convert this long internal note into a one-page brief for the Secretary.
Sections: Problem (3 lines) / Current Status / Decisions Required This Week.
Keep it under 250 words; keep all numbers exact.

Best for: 10+ page reports that need executive summary

4. Stakeholder Map Builder

Draft a stakeholder map for this policy [describe].
List Responsible / Accountable / Supporting / Blocking actors.
Add one sentence on each actor’s likely motivation.

Extension: Ask “What coalition-building steps would you recommend?”

5. Bias & Risk Checker

Review the following draft.
Mark any unsourced numbers or statements that could sound political.
Suggest neutral alternatives and note where data is missing.

Use before: Sending any note to Chief Secretary or Cabinet

⚕ Section 2: Health Policy Prompts

6. Primary-Care Gap Analysis

You are advising Punjab Health Dept. Identify 4 common barriers to primary-care access in urban slums and 1 low-cost, 6-month solution for each barrier that can be implemented by existing staff.

Add context: “Consider Lahore’s katchi abadis specifically” for targeted advice

7. Emergency Brief Generator

Draft a 1-page “Smog Emergency Brief” for Minister Health.
Include: current status (bullets) / 3 actions taken / 3 public instructions / 1 request to another department.
Tone: calm, factual, non-political.

Adapt for: Dengue, heatwave, or any health emergency

8. Frontline Worker Voice Simulation

Act as a Lady Health Worker in South Punjab.
Describe, in first person, 3 daily barriers to your work, how data reporting affects you, and what recognition or support would motivate you.

Value: Helps design worker-friendly policies from ground perspective

🎓 Section 3: Education Policy Prompts

9. Girls’ Drop-out Drill-Down

You are in the School Education Dept.
List 5 reasons girls drop out after class 5 in South Punjab.
For each reason give one actionable, 12-month intervention the department can do and which partner department is needed.

Follow-up: “Now create a monitoring framework for these interventions”

10. Foundational Learning KPIs

Suggest 2 indicators for access, 2 for learning, and 1 for teacher presence that can be tracked monthly by a district education authority.
For each KPI, note a realistic data source.

Specify: “Focus on primary schools in rural districts”

11. Parent Communication Rewriter

Rewrite this school-closure notice so a parent with grade-6 literacy can understand.
Use Urdu-English mix, short sentences (< 15 words), and friendly tone.

Test it: Share with non-officer staff to verify clarity

🧩 Section 4: Operations, Admin & Ethics

12. Meeting Minutes Generator

You are a section officer writing minutes from this meeting [insert notes].
Format: Participants / Agenda / Decisions / Responsible Officer + Deadline.
Tone: formal & neutral.

Save time: Record key points during meeting, let AI format properly

13. Circular Clarity Checker

Review this draft circular.
Rewrite so that required action, responsible officer, deadline, and consequences are clearly stated.
List 3 phrases that might confuse district staff.

Result: Fewer follow-up queries from field offices

14. ADP Timeline Planner

For this project [describe], draft a 6-month timeline with monthly milestones, responsible units, and main risk each month.
Assume typical Punjab approval and procurement delays.

Realistic touch: “Include buffer time for interdepartmental clearances”

15. Ethics & Privacy Guard

List 5 steps I should take before pasting internal government data into an AI tool.
Explain how to anonymize or summarize sensitive information (names, CNICs, patient data) safely.

Golden rule: Never paste citizen data directly—summarize or anonymize first

🛡️ Safe AI Use Principles

  1. Never paste citizen names, CNICs, or medical records
  2. Always verify AI-generated facts and numbers
  3. Use AI as assistant, not decision-maker
  4. Keep human judgment in all policy decisions
  5. Document when AI was used in any formal note
