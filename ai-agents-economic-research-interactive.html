🌐 Open Protocols (MCP & A2A) ▼

New standards enabling agent interoperability:

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Connects agents to resources, tools, and data

  • Released by Anthropic (Nov 2024)
  • ~10,000 MCP servers available
  • Like USB-C for AI agents
  • Access to Gmail, databases, FRED, IMF data

Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A)

Enables agents to communicate with each other

  • Released by Google (Apr 2025)
  • Specialized agents can collaborate
  • Coordinate on complex tasks
  • Now managed by Linux Foundation

The Reality Check

⚠️ These Agents Aren't Perfect

Think of them as research assistants, not independent researchers

🎭 Hallucinations & Errors ▼

The Problem: AI confidently makes up plausible-sounding information

Examples from the paper:
  • Fake statistics and citations
  • Outdated model references presented as current
  • Overconfident claims ("rapidly transforming" when adoption is still limited)
  • Sometimes generates pseudo-data that looks real but isn't

What to do:

  • Always verify outputs, especially data and citations
  • Ask for sources explicitly
  • Cross-check claims against authoritative sources
  • Maintain healthy skepticism
🧠 Limited Economic Reasoning ▼

Current agents struggle with:

  • Genuinely creative economic thinking
  • Identifying truly novel research questions
  • Recognizing the most impactful papers in emerging fields
  • Maintaining rigorous economic logic under pressure
  • Misapplying theoretical frameworks
  • Reproducing common misconceptions from training data
🎯 Bottom Line: They excel at synthesis and implementation, but not yet at the spark of insight that identifies profound research directions.
⚙️ Technical Brittleness ▼

Workflow Fragility:

  • Single failed step can halt entire process
  • Authentication walls stop progress
  • Small prompt variations = drastically different results
  • Evaluation and reproducibility challenges

Computational Cascades:

  • Errors can propagate through multi-agent systems
  • Tool failures may go undetected
  • Incomplete data gets incorporated into final outputs
⚠️ Security Risk: Prompt injection attacks can manipulate agent behavior through hidden instructions (see Gans 2025 on peer review manipulation).
